# Marketing home

Mode: Persuade. Audience: consultant/implementer and agent power user. Job: believe the same-day loop (server → tool → agent URL) and Sign up. Proof: the gate cut itself — a noisy REST catalog reduced to the tools that pass. Constraints: brand manual (Ink, Paper, Gate Orange on the 2 only, Inter, system mono, custom wordmark); no invented testimonials, metrics, customers, or shipped-roadmap; copy meaning may change, section order may change. Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/decision/gate-cut.webp`.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home is the gate. A wide REST catalog is cut by the stencil 2 into the few MCP tools that pass; Sign up sits in the opening. It refuses the centered SaaS hero, subtitle, twin CTAs, and feature-card stack.

OWN-WORLD: Paper `#FFFFFF` ground, Ink `#0B0B0B` type, Gate Orange `#FD410B` only on the 2. Inter for chrome (Bold titles, Regular body, Medium controls). System mono for endpoints and tool names. Sharp rectangles, no glow, glass, or drop shadow. Official wordmark lockup in the nav; the isolated 2 is the page’s instrument.

STORY: The visitor sees selection happen, believes they can host a small trustworthy MCP the same day, and Signs up (or Logs in).

FIRST VIEWPORT: Thin nav — wordmark left, Log in right. Three columns: left, a dense gray mono REST inventory sheared by the 2; center, a giant Gate Orange stencil 2 with Sign up in the horizontal opening; right, six Ink mono tool names that passed. No footer in the first screen.

FORM: The gate cut — grounded list position 2 of 7 (workbench, gate cut, connection slip, cull list, playground verdict, client handoff, two doors). Seed `59744ca2`.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
