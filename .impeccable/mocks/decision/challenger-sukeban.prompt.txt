High-fidelity designed website UI mockup of the rest2mcp marketing home first viewport. Desktop landscape, 1440 by 900 pixels, 16:9. Flat Paper white #FFFFFF page. Precise light instrument document, not a poster, not a fashion plate, not a photograph.

STRUCTURE, regions in order with scale:

1. NAV — thin full-width top bar, about 56px tall, Paper ground. Left: the exact custom rest2mcp wordmark from the attached reference image, used as-is. Lowercase stencil lockup, letters Ink #0B0B0B, only the numeral 2 is Gate Orange #FD410B. Do not typeset the name in Inter or any other face. Do not recolor, stretch, tilt, or close the stencil cuts. Right: small Inter condensed labels in Ink: Docs, Log in. Hairline Ink rule under the bar. No hamburger, no icons, no orange except the 2.

2. SPEC TITLE — left of the main field, compact. Inter condensed uppercase tracked label: KEEP / CUT. One short Inter regular sentence in Ink: A REST catalog crossed to curated MCP tools an agent can trust. No prices, no testimonials, no dates, no speed claims.

3. INVENTORY — the dominant region, left-center, roughly 58 percent of the width and most of the remaining height. A measured keep/cut specification sheet: a vertical inventory of endpoint rows in system monospace. Each row is a method, a path, a KEEP or CUT mark in Inter condensed, and for kept rows an agent-facing tool name. Example rows, real product logic only:
GET /invoices/{id}    KEEP    get_invoice
GET /invoices         KEEP    list_invoices
GET /customers/{id}   KEEP    get_customer
GET /balance          KEEP    get_balance
POST /invoices        CUT
PUT /invoices/{id}    CUT
PATCH /invoices/{id}  CUT
DELETE /invoices/{id} CUT
POST /webhooks        CUT
GET /health           CUT
Kept rows are full solid Ink, high contrast. Cut rows are struck through with a thin Ink line and recede to a lighter Ink gray. No decorative icons. Hairline column rules like a measured spec. A small mono count at the column head: 50 cap · 4 kept.

4. LEADER LINES AND RULES — right third of the page. Thin precise Ink leader lines, technical-drawing callouts, leave the specific inventory rows and end at short rule notes. Three rules only, system mono bodies, Inter condensed titles:
From the POST PUT PATCH DELETE cut cluster: Mutation guard — POST, PUT, PATCH, DELETE stay blocked until the owner opts in.
From the 50 cap count: 50 tools per server.
From a kept tool name such as get_invoice: Named for agents.
Callouts are flush annotations on Paper, not cards, not speech bubbles, no drop shadows, no orange markers.

5. SIGN UP — a compact solid Ink rectangle with Paper type Sign up, sitting on the last kept row (get_balance), aligned to that row, not a hero band, not centered in empty space.

GROUND AND MATERIAL: flat Paper #FFFFFF. Light working document. Generous margins. Hairline Ink rules. Inter condensed for labels. System monospace for endpoints and rules. Custom wordmark only from the reference.

COLOR LOCK: Paper #FFFFFF, Ink #0B0B0B, Gate Orange #FD410B exclusively on the wordmark 2. No other orange. No navy. No blue serge. No sailor uniforms. No embroidered stripes. No school uniforms. No people. No skirts. No fashion photography. No decorative illustration. No textures, gradients, photos, or chrome.
